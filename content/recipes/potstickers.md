## Ingredients

### Filling

- 1 tablespoon peanut or canola oil
- 1 1/2 cups green cabbage, finely chopped
- 2 tablespoons ginger, minced
- 2 tablespoons garlic, minced
- 1/4 cup scallions, chopped
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt, divided
- 1 pound 80% lean ground pork
- 2 teaspoons tapioca starch
- 3 tablespoons water
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 1 1/2 teaspoons sesame oil
- 1 teaspoon sugar

### Dumplings

- 35–40 round dumpling wrappers
- Neutral oil (peanut, canola, or vegetable oil) for frying
- 1/4 cup water (for steaming during cooking)

### Sweet Chili Sauce

- 1/2 cup honey
- 1/2 cup water
- 2 tablespoons rice vinegar
- 1 1/2 teaspoons cornstarch
- 1 clove garlic, minced (or 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder)
- 1/2–3/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes
- 1/2 teaspoon salt

## Instructions

### 1. Make the filling

1. Heat 1 tablespoon oil in a large pan over medium heat.
2. Add cabbage, ginger, garlic, and scallions. Cook for about 2 minutes, stirring frequently.
3. Add 1/4 teaspoon salt, stir, then remove from heat and let cool for 10 minutes.
4. In a large bowl, add ground pork and mix in tapioca starch and water.
5. Stir vigorously until the meat becomes slightly paste-like.
6. Add soy sauce, sesame oil, remaining salt, and sugar. Mix well.
7. Add cooled vegetables and combine thoroughly.

### 2. Form the dumplings

8. Set up dumpling wrappers, filling, and a small bowl of water (if using store-bought wrappers).
9. Place 1 to 1½ tablespoons of filling in each wrapper.
10. Fold and pleat dumplings into your preferred shape, sealing tightly.
11. Place finished dumplings on a tray and keep covered to prevent drying.

### 3. Cook the dumplings

12. Heat 1½ tablespoons oil in a large nonstick or cast iron pan over medium-high heat.
13. Add dumplings in a single layer and fry for 2–3 minutes until bottoms are golden brown.
14. Carefully add 1/4 cup water and immediately cover the pan.
15. Reduce heat to medium and steam for 5–6 minutes.
16. Remove lid and cook for 1 more minute until water evaporates.
17. Transfer dumplings to a plate and repeat if needed.

### 4. Make the sweet chili sauce

18. In a small saucepan, combine honey, water, rice vinegar, cornstarch, garlic, red pepper flakes, and salt.
19. Bring to a simmer over medium heat, stirring until slightly thickened.
20. Remove from heat and let cool slightly before serving.
