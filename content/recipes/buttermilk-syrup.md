## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 Cups white sugar
- 3/4 Cups buttermilk
- 1/2 Cups butter
- 2 Tablespoons corn syrup
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract

## Directions

1. In a saucepan, stir together the sugar, buttermilk, butter, corn syrup, and baking soda
2. Bring to a boil and cook for 7 minutes
3. Remove from the heat and stir in the vanilla

## Notes

We usually half this recipe, make sure to use a big pot!
