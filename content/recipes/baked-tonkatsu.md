## Ingredients

### Tonkatsu

- ¾ cup panko (Japanese breadcrumbs)
- 1 tablespoon neutral oil (for toasting panko)
- 2 boneless pork loin chops (½-inch thick, about ½ lb / 227 g total)
- ½ teaspoon kosher salt (Diamond Crystal or similar)
- Freshly ground black pepper, to taste
- 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
- 1 large egg
- ½ tablespoon neutral oil (for egg wash)
- Tonkatsu sauce, for serving

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C) and position a rack in the middle. Place a wire rack on a baking sheet.
2. In a skillet over medium heat, add panko and 1 tablespoon oil. Toast, stirring frequently, until golden brown. Transfer to a shallow dish and let cool.
3. Trim excess fat from pork chops and make small slits in the connective tissue to prevent curling during cooking.
4. Pound the pork lightly with a meat mallet or the back of a knife to an even thickness, then reshape as needed.
5. Season both sides with salt and black pepper.
6. In a shallow dish, beat the egg with ½ tablespoon oil.
7. Place flour in a separate shallow dish.
8. Dredge each pork chop in flour, shaking off excess.
9. Dip into the egg mixture, coating fully.
10. Press into toasted panko, coating thoroughly and firmly pressing crumbs onto the surface.
11. Place coated pork chops on the prepared wire rack.
12. Bake for about 20 minutes, until cooked through and golden.
13. Let rest for 3 minutes before slicing.
14. Slice into 1-inch strips by pressing straight down with a knife.
15. Serve immediately with tonkatsu sauce.
