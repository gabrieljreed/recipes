## Ingredients

- 12 oz pasta
- 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- 1 medium yellow onion, chopped
- 1 lb Italian sausage
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- ½ bunch kale (about 4–5 oz), stems removed and leaves chopped
- Red pepper flakes, to taste
- Kosher salt, to taste
- ½ cup heavy cream
- 1 cup finely grated Parmigiano Reggiano

## Instructions

1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook pasta until al dente. Reserve 1 cup of pasta water before draining.
2. While the pasta cooks, heat olive oil in a large skillet or Dutch oven over medium-high heat.
3. Add the onion and cook for 3–4 minutes, until softened.
4. Add the Italian sausage and break it apart with a spoon. Cook until browned and cooked through.
5. Add garlic, kale, red pepper flakes, and salt. Cook for about 2 minutes, stirring frequently, until kale is wilted.
6. Add the cooked pasta and 1/4 cup of reserved pasta water. Toss to combine.
7. Remove from heat and stir in the heavy cream and Parmigiano Reggiano.
8. Add more pasta water if needed to loosen the sauce.
9. Taste and adjust salt, then serve immediately.
