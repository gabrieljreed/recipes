## Ingredients

### Chicken

- 2 pounds boneless, skinless chicken thighs

### Marinade 1

- 1 tablespoon kosher salt
- 1 tablespoon red chili powder
- 6 garlic cloves, peeled and mashed into a paste
- 1 (1-inch) piece ginger, peeled and mashed into a paste
- Juice of 2 lemons

### Marinade 2

- 1 cup (225 g) plain yogurt
- ¼ cup (60 ml) canola oil
- 1 tablespoon garam masala
- 2 teaspoons ground turmeric

### Gravy

- ½ red onion, diced
- ½ cup tomato paste
- 2½–3 cups water
- Handful of cashews
- 5–8 garlic cloves
- 2 tablespoons malt vinegar (or 1½ tablespoons white vinegar)
- ½ teaspoon garam masala
- ½–1 teaspoon chili powder
- 1 clove
- 1 cardamom pod
- 1 teaspoon kasoori methi
- Honey, to taste
- 5 tablespoons butter
- 3 tablespoons cream
- Salt, to taste

## Instructions

1. Place the chicken in a large bowl and toss with the kosher salt, 1 teaspoon of the chili powder, half of the garlic paste (3 cloves), the lemon juice, and half of the ginger paste (½ inch). Let sit at room temperature for 10 minutes.
2. In a medium bowl, whisk together the remaining chili powder, remaining garlic paste, remaining ginger paste, yogurt, canola oil, garam masala, and turmeric.
3. Add the chicken and toss to coat. Cover and refrigerate for at least 6 hours, preferably overnight.
4. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
5. Grease a rack fitted in a sheet tray with 2 tablespoons melted butter. Arrange the chicken on the rack and bake for about 13 minutes, or until cooked through.
6. Let the chicken cool slightly, then cut into 2-inch pieces and set aside.
7. In a pan, melt a spoonful of butter and cook the diced onion until softened.
8. Add the tomato paste, cashews, water, garlic, salt, vinegar, garam masala, chili powder, clove, and cardamom pod. Stir well and simmer for 15–20 minutes. Alternatively, cook in a slow cooker throughout the day.
9. Blend the mixture into a smooth puree and return it to the pan.
10. Add honey to taste.
11. Stir in the remaining butter, cream, cooked chicken, and kasoori methi. Simmer for 5–7 minutes.
12. Garnish with additional cream and kasoori methi before serving.
