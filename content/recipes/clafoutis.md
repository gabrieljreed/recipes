## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 pounds fresh sweet cherries, pitted and halved (or drained canned cherries), or blueberries
- 1 teaspoon lemon juice (if using fresh cherries or berries)
- 2 teaspoons all-purpose flour (for fruit coating)
- 1/8 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 4 large eggs
- 2/3 cup sugar
- 2 1/2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 2/3 cup whole milk
- 1 tablespoon unsalted butter
- Powdered sugar, for dusting

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C). Place a 12-inch oven-safe skillet in the oven on a lower rack. Line a baking sheet with foil for easy cleanup.
2. Toss cherries with lemon juice in a medium bowl.
3. Sprinkle cherries with 2 teaspoons flour and cinnamon, then toss to coat.
4. In a large bowl, whisk eggs, sugar, vanilla, and salt until pale and smooth, about 1 minute.
5. Whisk in 1/2 cup flour until fully combined.
6. Add milk and heavy cream and whisk until smooth.
7. Carefully remove the hot skillet from the oven and add the butter, swirling until melted and coating the pan.
8. Immediately pour batter into the skillet.
9. Evenly distribute cherries over the batter (some will sink).
10. Bake for 18–22 minutes, rotating halfway through, until puffed and golden and the center is set.
11. Remove and cool for 25 minutes.
12. Dust with powdered sugar and serve sliced into wedges.
