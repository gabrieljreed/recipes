## Ingredients

- 10 lasagna noodles, broken into bite-size pieces
- 1 Tbsp olive oil
- 1 lb mild or spicy Italian sausage, casings removed
- ½ large onion, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 tsp red pepper flakes (optional)
- 28 oz can San Marzano tomatoes, crushed
- ½ cup hot water
- 8 oz tomato sauce
- 2–3 oz fresh Parmesan cheese, grated
- 8 oz mozzarella cheese, cubed
- Ricotta cheese, to taste
- Fresh basil, chopped

## Instructions

1. Break the lasagna noodles into bite-size pieces.
2. Heat the olive oil in a large pot. Add the sausage and cook until browned and crumbled, removing it from the pot while the centers are still slightly pink.
3. Add the onion and cook for 3–5 minutes, until softened.
4. Add the garlic and red pepper flakes (if using) and cook for 30 seconds, until fragrant.
5. Add the crushed tomatoes, cooked sausage, hot water, broken noodles, and tomato sauce.
6. Simmer for about 20 minutes, stirring every 5 minutes, until the noodles are cooked.
7. Stir in the grated Parmesan and cubed mozzarella cheese.
8. Serve topped with ricotta cheese and fresh basil.
