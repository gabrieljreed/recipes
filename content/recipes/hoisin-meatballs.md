## Ingredients

### Meatballs

- 1 pound ground beef
- 1 teaspoon toasted sesame oil
- 1/2 cup panko breadcrumbs
- 1/4 teaspoon ground ginger
- 1 large egg
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 3 green onions, thinly sliced (plus more for garnish)
- Sesame seeds, for garnish

### Hoisin Sauce

- 1/4 cup hoisin sauce
- 2 tablespoons rice vinegar
- 1 tablespoon soy sauce
- 1/2 teaspoon toasted sesame oil
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/2 teaspoon ground ginger

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment paper or a silicone mat.
2. In a large bowl, combine ground beef, sesame oil, panko, ground ginger, egg, garlic, and green onions.
3. Mix until well combined, then form into 1¼–1½ inch meatballs (about 18–20 total).
4. Place meatballs on the prepared baking sheet.
5. Bake for about 12 minutes, until browned and cooked through.
6. While the meatballs bake, whisk together hoisin sauce, rice vinegar, soy sauce, sesame oil, garlic, and ginger in a small bowl.
7. Serve meatballs hot, drizzled or dipped in hoisin sauce.
8. Garnish with extra green onions and sesame seeds.
