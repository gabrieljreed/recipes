## Ingredients

- 4 baking potatoes (about 2 1/2 pounds)
- 2/3 cup all-purpose flour
- 6 cups 2% milk
- 1 cup reduced-fat shredded extra-sharp cheddar cheese, divided
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 cup reduced-fat sour cream
- 3/4 cup chopped green onions, divided
- 6 bacon slices, cooked and crumbled

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
2. Pierce potatoes with a fork and bake for about 1 hour, until tender. Let cool.
3. Peel potatoes and coarsely mash them, discarding the skins.
4. Lightly spoon flour into a dry measuring cup and level it off.
5. In a large Dutch oven, whisk flour into the milk gradually until smooth.
6. Cook over medium heat, stirring frequently, until thick and bubbly (about 8 minutes).
7. Stir in mashed potatoes, 3/4 cup cheddar cheese, salt, and pepper until the cheese melts.
8. Remove from heat and stir in sour cream and 1/2 cup green onions.
9. Cook over low heat for about 10 minutes until heated through (do not boil).
10. Serve topped with remaining cheese, green onions, and crumbled bacon.
