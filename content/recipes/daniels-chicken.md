## Ingredients

### Marinade

- 4 chicken thighs
- 2 T olive oil
- 2 T tomato paste
- 4-5 minced garlic cloves
- Zest and juice of 1 large lemon
- Salt & pepper
- 1 T smoked paprika
- 1 T oregano
- 1/4-1/2 tsp cayenne pepper

### Sauce

- 3 T greek yogurt
- 3 T fresh mint
- 2 scallions
- 1/2 large jalapeno
- 1 T olice oil
- Juice from 1/2 lemon
- Salt & pepper

## Directions

- Grab 4 chicken thighs, dab dry with a paper towel and put inside a bowl.
- For the marinade, add 2 T olive oil, 2 T tomato paste, 4-5 minced garlic cloves, the zest and juice of 1 large lemon, a couple generous pinches salt, fresh cracked pepper, 1 T smoked paprika, 1 T oregano, 1/4-1/2 tsp cayenne pepper.
- Mix with the chicken really well until all sides are covered. Let sit for 2-6 hrs, or ideally overnight
- Preheat oven to 410F. Remove chicken from fridge and let it come to room temp before putting it in the oven (about 30 min).
- Place chicken on a wire rack on a baking sheet and bake it at 410 for 35-40 min.
- While your chicken is going, make the sauce. Process 3 T thick greek yogurt, a handful of fresh mint (3 T), 2 scallions (green and white part), 1/2 large jalapeño, 1 T olive oil, juice of half a lemon, and a couple generous pinches of salt and pepper. Blend well, taste, and adjust as you like.
- Serve over rice
