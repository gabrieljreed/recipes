## Ingredients

- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 3 cups fennel, chopped (1 large bulb)
- 1½ cups yellow onion, chopped
- 1¼ pounds sweet Italian sausage, casings removed
- 2 teaspoons garlic, minced (2 cloves)
- ½ teaspoon whole fennel seeds, crushed
- ½ teaspoon crushed red pepper flakes
- 2 teaspoons kosher salt, plus more for pasta water
- 1 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
- 1 cup dry white wine
- 1 cup heavy cream
- ⅔ cup half-and-half
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 1 pound rigatoni
- ½ cup fresh parsley, chopped
- 1 cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese, divided

## Instructions

1. Heat the olive oil in a large heavy pot or Dutch oven over medium heat.
2. Add the fennel and onion and sauté for about 7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until tender.
3. Add the sausage and cook for 7–8 minutes, breaking it up with a spoon, until browned.
4. Add the garlic, crushed fennel seeds, red pepper flakes, 2 teaspoons salt, and black pepper. Cook for 1 minute.
5. Pour in the white wine and bring to a boil.
6. Add the heavy cream, half-and-half, and tomato paste. Stir until combined.
7. Return to a boil, then reduce the heat and simmer for 20 minutes, until the sauce has thickened.
8. Meanwhile, bring a large pot of water to a boil and add 2 tablespoons salt.
9. Cook the rigatoni according to the package directions, then drain.
10. Add the cooked rigatoni to the sauce and stir to coat.
11. Cook over low heat for 5 minutes, allowing the pasta to absorb some of the sauce.
12. Remove from the heat and stir in the parsley and ½ cup of the Parmesan cheese.
13. Serve hot with the remaining ½ cup Parmesan cheese on the side.
