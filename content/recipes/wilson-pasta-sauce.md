## Ingredients

- 2 T olive oil
- 2 T butter
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 4 cloves garlic
- 2 15oz cans tomato sauce
- Salt & Pepper
- Dash of sugar
- 1 C cream
- Parmesan

## Directions

1. Heat butter and oil on medium heat
2. Add onions and garlic, stir 1 minute
3. Add sauce, s&p, sugar, stir over low heat 25-30 minutes
4. Remove from heat, add cream and parm to taste
