## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons yeast
- 1/2 cup warm water
- 2 cups hot water
- 1 tablespoon salt
- 6 cups all-purpose flour (divided)
- 3 tablespoons sugar
- 1/3 cup butter, melted
- 1 teaspoon dried basil (optional)
- 1 cup grated Parmesan or Asiago cheese (optional)
- Whole wheat flour (optional substitute for up to 1/2 the flour)

## Instructions

1. Dissolve yeast in 1/2 cup warm water and set aside.
2. In a stand mixer bowl, combine hot water, salt, 3 cups flour, and sugar. Beat well until smooth.
3. Add the yeast mixture and mix to combine.
4. Add melted butter and mix again.
5. Add the remaining 3 cups flour and beat until smooth. (Optional: add dried basil and grated cheese during this step.)
6. Leave the dough in the mixer and let it rest, stirring down every 10 minutes for 50 minutes total.
7. Turn dough out onto a floured surface and divide into two equal portions.
8. Roll each portion into a rectangle about the length of a French bread pan.
9. Roll up jelly-roll style, tucking ends underneath to seal.
10. Place loaves into French bread pans and let rise for 20 minutes.
11. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
12. Slash each loaf diagonally four times just before baking.
13. Bake for 25 minutes, until golden brown.
14. Cool in pans for a few minutes, then transfer to a wire rack to cool completely.
