## Ingredients

### Chicken

- 4 chicken drumsticks (~150 g / 5 oz each)
- 4 chicken thighs, skin-on and bone-in (~250 g / 8 oz each)
- 1 tsp salt (kosher or cooking salt)
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 4 tbsp unsalted butter

### Stew

- 300 g (10 oz) white mushrooms, halved or quartered
- 2 medium brown onions, sliced (about 0.6 cm / 1/2 in thick)
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 bay leaf (fresh or dried)
- 3 thyme sprigs (or 1/2 tsp dried thyme)
- 3 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 1/2 cup white wine (preferably Chardonnay)
- 3 cups chicken stock (low sodium)
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
- 2/3 cup heavy cream

## Instructions

1. Pat the chicken dry and season all over with salt and black pepper.
2. Melt the butter in a large skillet or heavy pot with a lid over medium-high heat.
3. Add the chicken thighs, skin side down, and cook for 4–5 minutes until golden brown. Flip and cook for 1 more minute, then remove to a plate.
4. Brown the drumsticks on multiple sides, about 2 minutes per side, then remove from the pan.
5. Add the mushrooms, onions, bay leaf, and thyme to the pan. Cook for about 5 minutes until lightly golden.
6. Add the garlic and cook for 30 seconds.
7. Stir in the flour and cook for 1 minute.
8. Pour in the white wine and chicken stock, then add salt and pepper. Scrape the bottom of the pan to release any browned bits.
9. Return the chicken to the pot, skin side up.
10. Bring to a simmer, then reduce heat so it is gently bubbling. Cover and cook for 10 minutes.
11. Uncover and continue simmering for 20 minutes, until the chicken is fully cooked.
12. Remove the chicken to a plate.
13. Stir the cream into the sauce and bring back to a gentle simmer. Adjust salt to taste.
14. Return the chicken to the sauce.
15. Sprinkle with parsley and serve, ideally over mashed potatoes, rice, or short pasta.
