## Ingredients

### Bulgogi Beef

- 500 g (1 lb) ground beef
- 1 tablespoon canola oil (or other neutral oil)

### Bulgogi Sauce

- 1 tablespoon onion, finely grated (about 1/4 onion)
- 3 tablespoons red apple, finely grated (about 1/2 apple, skin on is fine)
- 3 garlic cloves, finely grated
- 1 teaspoon ginger, finely grated
- 2½ tablespoons soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon sesame oil
- 1 tablespoon mirin
- 1 tablespoon brown sugar
- 1/8 teaspoon black pepper

### Rice Bowl Drizzle Sauce

- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon sesame oil
- 2 teaspoons brown sugar
- 2 teaspoons rice vinegar (or apple cider vinegar)

### Rice Bowl

- White rice, cooked
- 4 large handfuls baby spinach
- 2 teaspoons canola oil
- 1 large carrot, peeled and julienned or grated
- 1 avocado, mashed
- 1/4 cup pickled ginger
- 1½ cups romaine lettuce, torn into bite-size pieces
- 1 teaspoon white sesame seeds
- 1 green onion, finely sliced

## Instructions

1. In a bowl, combine the grated onion, grated apple, garlic, ginger, soy sauce, sesame oil, mirin, brown sugar, and black pepper to make the bulgogi sauce.
2. In a separate bowl, whisk together the soy sauce, sesame oil, brown sugar, and rice vinegar to make the drizzle sauce.
3. Heat 2 teaspoons of oil in a large non-stick pan over high heat.
4. Add half of the spinach and toss until partially wilted, about 30 seconds.
5. Add the remaining spinach and continue tossing until fully wilted, about 1 minute total. Transfer to a bowl and set aside.
6. Add 1 tablespoon oil to the same pan.
7. Add the ground beef and cook over high heat, breaking it apart as it cooks, until no raw meat remains.
8. Pour in the bulgogi sauce and simmer, stirring occasionally, until most of the sauce has evaporated.
9. Continue cooking and stirring until the beef develops some caramelization while remaining moist.
10. Divide cooked rice among serving bowls.
11. Top with the bulgogi beef, wilted spinach, and carrot.
12. Add a dollop of mashed avocado and the pickled ginger.
13. Arrange the lettuce alongside the other toppings.
14. Sprinkle with sesame seeds and sliced green onion.
15. Serve with the drizzle sauce for spooning over the bowls before eating.
