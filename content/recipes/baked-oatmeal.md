## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 cups milk (preferably not skim)
- 2 large eggs
- 1/2 cup maple syrup or packed light brown sugar
- 1/4 cup butter, melted
- 1 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 1 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 3 cups rolled oats (quick oats also work)

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Butter an 8x8-inch baking dish.
2. In a large mixing bowl, whisk together milk, eggs, maple syrup or brown sugar, melted butter, baking powder, vanilla, cinnamon, and salt.
3. Stir in the oats until fully combined.
4. Pour mixture into the prepared baking dish and spread evenly.
5. Bake for 30–40 minutes, until set.
6. Let cool slightly, then cut into squares and serve warm.
7. Optional: Serve with fruit, nuts, milk, cream, maple syrup, or honey.
8. Optional: For make-ahead, refrigerate overnight, then let sit at room temperature for 20 minutes before baking.
