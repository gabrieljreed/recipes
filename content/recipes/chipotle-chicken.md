## Ingredients

- 1 tsp cumin
- 4 garlic cloves
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 2 tsp kosher salt
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 tbsp chili powder
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 7oz can chipotle peppers in adobo sauce (only use 4 peppers and all sauce)
- 1/2 cup water (takes out flavor if 3/4)

## Instructions

- Blend ingredients

## Notes

4 peppers was a bit spicy
