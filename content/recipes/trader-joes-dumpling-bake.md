## Ingredients

- 1 Can Coconut Cream
- 3tbsp Trader Joe’s Soyaki
- 1/2 Bottle Thai Style Red Curry Sauce
- 3-5 Cloves Minced Garlic
- 1tsp Sesame Oil
- 1 Pack Trader Joe’s Dumplings
- Crunchy chili onion (optional)
- Green Onions (optional for garnish)
- Black sesame seeds (optional for garnish)

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 400
2. Mix all ingredients and place dumplings in sauce
3. Cover with foil
4. Bake for 30-35 min.
5. Serve over steamed white rice
