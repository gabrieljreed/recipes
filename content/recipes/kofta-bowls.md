## Ingredients

### Rice

- 3/4 C long grain rice
- 1.5 C water
- 1 tsp chicken bouillon
- 1/2 tsp aleppo pepper
- 1/4 tsp turmeric powder
- 2 whole cloves

### Beef

- 1 lb ground beef
- 1/8-1/4 grated onion
- Handful of chopped parsley
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 tsp cumin
- 1/4 tsp ground allspice
- 1 tsp ground coriander

### Sauce

- 2/3 C Greek yogurt
- 1/4 C mayo
- 2 tsp dill
- 4-6 garlic cloves
- Splash of pickle juice or vinegar
- Black pepper

### Toppings

- Lettuce/spinach
- Feta
- Pickles
- Tomatoes
- Fresh/pickled onions

## Instructions

- Put rice and its ingredients in a rice cooker, give it a stir, and start it
- Cook ground beef on stovetop with all its spices
- Combine everything to make sauce. I keep adding garlic, pickle juice, and pepper to taste.
- Put it all in a bowl, add toppings, mix, and serve.
