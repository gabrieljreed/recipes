## Ingredients

### Meat Filling

- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 cup yellow onion, chopped
- 1 lb 90% lean ground beef or ground lamb
- 2 teaspoons dried parsley
- 1 teaspoon dried rosemary
- 1 teaspoon dried thyme
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon ground black pepper
- 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 1 cup beef broth
- 1 cup frozen peas and carrots
- 1/2 cup frozen corn kernels

### Potato Topping

- 1½–2 lb russet potatoes, peeled and cut into 1-inch cubes (about 2 large potatoes)
- 8 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 1/3 cup half-and-half
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon ground black pepper
- 1/4 cup Parmesan cheese

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 400°F (205°C).
2. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
3. Add the onion and cook for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally.
4. Add the ground beef or lamb and break it apart with a spoon.
5. Stir in the parsley, rosemary, thyme, salt, and pepper. Cook for 6–8 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the meat is browned.
6. Add the Worcestershire sauce and garlic. Cook for 1 minute.
7. Stir in the flour and tomato paste until well combined and no clumps remain.
8. Add the beef broth, peas and carrots, and corn. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer and cook for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally.
9. Remove the meat filling from the heat and set aside.
10. Place the potatoes in a large pot and cover with water.
11. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer and cook for 10–15 minutes, until the potatoes are fork-tender.
12. Drain the potatoes and return them to the hot pot. Let them sit for 1 minute to evaporate excess moisture.
13. Add the butter, half-and-half, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Mash until smooth and well combined.
14. Stir in the Parmesan cheese.
15. Transfer the meat filling to a 9×9-inch or 7×11-inch baking dish and spread into an even layer.
16. Spoon the mashed potatoes over the meat filling and carefully spread into an even layer.
17. If the dish is very full, place it on a rimmed baking sheet to catch any overflow.
18. Bake uncovered for 25–30 minutes.
19. Let cool for 15 minutes before serving.
