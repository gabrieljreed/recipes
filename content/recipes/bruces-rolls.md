## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon yeast
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 1 cup water, warmed
- 1/3 cup butter, melted
- 1 cup milk, warmed
- 1 large egg
- 4 1/2 cups bread flour (divided)
- 1 tablespoon salt

## Instructions

1. In a stand mixer bowl, add yeast, sugar, and warm water. Do not mix. Let sit while preparing next step.
2. In a microwave-safe measuring cup, melt the butter, then add the milk and warm together.
3. Add the egg to the yeast mixture.
4. Pour in the butter and milk mixture. Do not mix yet.
5. Add 3 cups of bread flour and mix with the dough hook for 2 minutes. The mixture will be very wet.
6. Add the remaining 1 1/2 cups bread flour and the salt.
7. Mix with the dough hook for another 3–4 minutes until combined.
8. Cover the bowl and let the dough rise for about 1 hour, or until doubled in size.
9. The dough will still be sticky. Lightly flour your hands and form into rolls.
10. Place rolls on a baking sheet and let them rise again while the oven preheats.
11. Bake at 400°F (200°C) for 10–11 minutes, until golden brown.
12. Makes about 24 rolls depending on size.
