## Ingredients

### Chicken Marinade

- 2 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs

#### Marinade 1

- 1 tbsp kosher salt
- 1 inch ginger, mashed into a paste
- Juice of 2 lemons
- 1 tsp red chili powder
- 3 garlic cloves, mashed into a paste

#### Marinade 2

- 1 cup plain yogurt
- 1/4 cup canola oil
- 1 tbsp garam masala
- 2 tsp ground turmeric
- 1 tsp red chili powder
- 3 garlic cloves, mashed into a paste


### Makhni Gravy

- 1 lb (450 g) tomatoes, halved
- 1 garlic clove, mashed into paste
- 1/2 inch ginger, mashed into paste
- 1 tsp red chili powder
- 1 small green chili (optional)
- 1 cardamom pod
- 1 whole clove
- 15 g cilantro (stems included)
- Salt, to taste
- 1/2 tbsp unsalted butter
- 1/4 cup heavy cream
- 2 tbsp kasoori methi
- Honey, to taste

### Onion Tomato Masala

- 1/4 cup canola oil
- 1/2 lb red onion, diced
- 2 garlic cloves, mashed into paste
- 3/4 inch ginger, mashed into paste
- 1/2 tsp red chili powder
- 1/2 lb (225 g) tomatoes, diced
- 1/2 tsp garam masala
- Salt, to taste

### Final Chicken Tikka Masala

- 3 tbsp ghee or butter
- 3 tsp cumin seeds (or ground cumin)
- 8 dried red chilies (optional)
- 7 small garlic cloves, chopped
- Cooked chicken tikka (from above)
- 2 tsp red chili powder
- 1 1/4 cups onion tomato masala
- 1 1/4 cups makhni gravy
- 5 green chilies, thinly sliced (optional)
- 1/4 cup heavy cream
- 1 tbsp unsalted butter
- 2 tbsp kasoori methi
- 1 tbsp garam masala
- Salt, to taste
- Chopped cilantro, for garnish
- 1 inch ginger, julienned, for garnish

## Instructions

### 1. Marinate the chicken

1. In a large bowl, toss chicken with salt, 1 tsp chili powder, half the garlic paste, lemon juice, and half the ginger paste. Let sit for 10 minutes.
2. In a separate bowl, whisk together remaining chili powder, remaining garlic paste, remaining ginger paste, yogurt, oil, garam masala, and turmeric.
3. Add chicken and coat well. Cover and refrigerate at least 6 hours or overnight.

### 2. Cook the chicken tikka

4. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).
5. Place chicken on a greased rack over a sheet tray.
6. Brush with melted butter and bake until cooked through (about 13 minutes).
7. Let cool slightly, then cut into 2-inch pieces.

### 3. Make the makhni gravy

8. In a saucepan, combine tomatoes, garlic paste, ginger paste, chili powder, and 1.5 cups water.
9. Add cardamom, clove, green chili, and cilantro stems (wrapped in cheesecloth or loose, to be removed later).
10. Bring to a boil, then simmer for about 45 minutes until reduced slightly.
11. Remove spices and blend until smooth.
12. Return to pan and simmer until thickened (about 12 minutes).
13. Stir in butter, cream, kasoori methi, and honey to balance acidity. Adjust salt.

### 4. Make onion tomato masala

14. Heat oil in a pan over medium-high heat.
15. Cook onions until golden (about 10 minutes).
16. Add garlic, ginger, and chili powder. Reduce heat and cook 8 minutes.
17. Add tomatoes and cook until softened and oil separates (about 3 minutes).
18. Stir in garam masala and salt to taste.

### 5. Finish the curry

19. Heat ghee in a large pan over medium-high heat.
20. Add cumin seeds and dried chilies; cook until crackling.
21. Add garlic and cook until lightly golden.
22. Add cooked chicken tikka and stir for 1 minute.
23. Add remaining chili powder, onion tomato masala, and makhni gravy.
24. Add green chilies (if using) and simmer briefly until heated through.
25. Stir in cream, butter, garam masala, and kasoori methi. Adjust salt.

### 6. Serve

26. Garnish with chopped cilantro and julienned ginger.
27. Serve with steamed basmati rice.
