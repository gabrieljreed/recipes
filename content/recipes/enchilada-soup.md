## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 small white onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 4 cups chicken stock
- 2–3 cups shredded cooked chicken (about 1 lb)
- 1 1/4 cups (10 oz) enchilada sauce
- 1 (15 oz) can black beans, rinsed and drained
- 1 (15 oz) can fire-roasted diced tomatoes, with juices
- 1 (4 oz) can chopped green chiles
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1/2 cup masa harina
- 8 oz sharp cheddar cheese, freshly grated
- 4 oz cream cheese
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

### Optional toppings

- Tortilla chips or strips
- Diced avocado
- Extra shredded cheese
- Sour cream
- Chopped fresh cilantro
- Pico de gallo

## Instructions

1. Heat olive oil in a large stockpot over medium-high heat.
2. Add the onion and sauté for about 5 minutes, until softened.
3. Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute, stirring frequently.
4. Add chicken stock, cooked chicken, enchilada sauce, black beans, diced tomatoes, green chiles, and cumin. Stir to combine.
5. Bring the soup to a simmer, then reduce heat to medium-low to maintain a gentle simmer.
6. In a separate bowl, whisk masa harina with 2 cups of hot broth until smooth.
7. Stir the masa mixture back into the soup.
8. Add cheddar cheese and cream cheese, stirring until fully melted and combined.
9. Taste and season with salt and pepper as needed.
10. Serve warm with desired toppings.
